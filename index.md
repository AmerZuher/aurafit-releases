# AuraFit

A food diary and fitness tracker for Android, in English and Arabic: meals and daily goals, water and weight, workouts, meal plans and an AI coach. Your diary stays on your phone.

- **Download:** the latest version is on the [Releases page](https://github.com/AmerZuher/aurafit-releases/releases/latest) — `auraFit_V<version>.apk`. Once installed, AuraFit updates itself from there (Settings › App updates).
