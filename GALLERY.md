## 🎨 Color Palette & Design System

### Brand Colors

| Color | Hex | Usage |
|-------|-----|-------|
| **Primary Blue** | `#4ABCE2` | Accents, CTAs, highlights |
| **Dark Background** | `#0A1628` | Primary background |
| **Dark Accent** | `#1A3A52` | Secondary backgrounds |

### Design Principles

✨ **Calm & Clean** — Minimal, purposeful design
