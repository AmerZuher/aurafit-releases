# AuraFit privacy policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026. Contact: **amerzuher@outlook.com**

AuraFit is a food diary and fitness tracker made by Amer Alreyahi. This policy says what leaves your phone, when, and why. In short: **your diary stays on your phone.** Your account holds only who you are, your plan and your AI credits. Anything else leaves your phone only when you use a feature that needs it.

## What stays on your phone

Your food diary, meals, products and recipes, water, weight, notes, workouts, body measurements, meal plans, goals, profile (age, sex, height, activity, target) and settings are stored only on your phone. We don't have a copy unless you use cloud backup.

If you turn on Health Connect in AuraFit, it reads steps, active calories, exercise and sleep on your phone, after you allow each type in Health Connect. A copy is kept on your phone for Insights, and turning Health Connect off in AuraFit deletes it.

## Your account

You sign in with Google (or Microsoft). Your account, stored with our database provider Supabase, holds:

- your name and email address, as Google or Microsoft give them (you can change the name in Settings › Account);
- your plan (Free, Pro or VIP) and, for Pro, when it ends;
- how many AI credits you used this month.

Your Google or Microsoft profile photo is shown in the app, loaded from their servers. A photo you choose instead stays on your phone: it isn't uploaded or backed up.

We use these only to run your account. We don't use them for advertising, and we don't sell or share them.

## AI features

AI features send data only when you tap them. Each request goes to AuraFit's relay (run by us on Cloudflare), which checks your sign-in and credits and passes it to OpenRouter, which sends it to an AI model. The relay stores nothing and keeps no logs of what you send.

- **Estimate with AI:** the food name you typed, and its ingredients if you typed them.
- **Meal photo:** the photo you take or pick (made smaller first) and the food name if you typed one. The photo isn't saved.
- **AI coach (Insights):** only if you turn on "Share my data with AI": your age, sex, height, activity and target (never your name), your goals, and 14 days of daily totals, water, weight and workouts, plus steps, active calories, exercise and sleep if Health Connect is on. Food names, notes and meal names are never sent.
- **InBody sheet:** the photo or PDF you pick, after you confirm.
- **AI meal plan (Your week):** each kind of day's meals and times, eating window, goals and meal targets, your target, and the food preferences you typed.

A request carries your sign-in so your credits can be counted, and nothing else about you. OpenRouter and the AI model's provider process it only to produce the answer.

## Cloud backup (Pro and VIP)

With Pro or VIP, AuraFit can back up your diary, workouts, body measurements and settings to private storage (Supabase), daily or when you tap Back up now. Only your account can read it. You can encrypt it with a password on your phone first, and then nobody else can read it, us included. You can delete it any time in Settings › Account › Cloud backup.

## Subscriptions

Pro is paid through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card. RevenueCat, our subscription provider, records which account bought which subscription and until when.

## Other requests the app makes

- **Food search and barcodes:** the text or barcode goes to Open Food Facts and/or USDA FoodData Central. Each can be turned off in Settings.
- **Product links** you paste are downloaded from that site.
- **App updates** (only in the version from GitHub) check GitHub's public releases at most once a day.

None of these carry your account, diary or anything else about you.

## Advertising and tracking

AuraFit has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don't sell your data, and we share it with no one except the providers named above, who process it only for the feature you used.

## Keeping and deleting your data

- Your account stays until you delete it; your cloud backup until you delete it or your account.
- **In the app:** Settings › Account › Delete account deletes your account, AI credits and cloud backup at once. Your diary on your phone stays (uninstall AuraFit to remove it).
- **Without the app:** email amerzuher@outlook.com from the address you signed in with. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.
- Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Google Play subscription; cancel it in Google Play.

## Security

All requests use encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your sign-in is kept in your phone's secure storage, and database rules let each account read only its own data.

## Children

AuraFit is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly create accounts for them.

## Changes

When this policy changes, the app shows the new version and asks you to accept it before you continue.
